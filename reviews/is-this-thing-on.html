Movie Review - Is This Thing On? Written By Evan Turok Sometimes you have to take a risk and just hope the movie you're about to watch was worth leaving the house for. Sometimes it is; other times you exit the cinema, the sun blasting your unadjusted eyes with the glare of a thousand suns, wishing you could have your 20 dollars and 2 hours back. Luckily, it’s my job to take that risk for you. ‘Is This Thing On?’ lands somewhere in between. Will Arnett and Laura Dern play a struggling couple whose marriage is falling apart in real time. While trying to clear his head and avoid paying the fifteen-dollar cover charge, Arnett's character, Alex, reluctantly signs up for the open mic standup comedy show. Feeling the pressure of the crowd, Alex turns to making fun of his current situation, quickly realising that putting a comedic spin on his misfortunes is therapeutic, so he uses this outlet to try to build back his relationship. If you come to this movie expecting the world, you're going to be disappointed, but if you're just looking for a heartwarming and funny film, look no further. The biggest reason I love seeing movies in the cinema is for the atmosphere. I love sharing the experience with others; it was really refreshing to hear consistent, audible laughter from the people around me. Many actors try to make the transition from acting to directing, and although they may have years of experience in the film business, it’s very clear that ‘Is This Thing On?’ is Bradley Cooper’s directorial debut. Before new directors learn the ‘rules’ of film, there is a window of creativity and invitation that can occasionally lead to the birth of auteur directors whose personal styles take on a life of their own. Unfortunately, Cooper isn’t in this elite group. I found myself often feeling that a shot was repetitive or redundant, sometimes leaving me somewhat bored, this being the ultimate sin for any film. Finding a balance between comedy and drama is a fine line. ‘Is This Thing On?’ managed to walk that tightrope to an acceptable standard. If you are looking for something to watch that will leave you with a gentle smile, this is the movie for you, but don't go expecting to be blown away. Is This Thing On? Barely. 3/5 stars.
