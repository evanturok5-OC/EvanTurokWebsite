Movie review - It’s a Wonderful Life Written by Evan Turok In the lead up to Christmas, I rewatched not only the greatest Christmas movie but also one of my personal favorite films of all time; Frank Capra’s It’s a Wonderful Life (1944), It is the beautiful, emotional, and heartbreaking story of George Bailey (James Stewart), whose dreams and future are planned out in his head from a young age, but his own selfless nature throws off his plans of seeing the world. It’s a film that shows us that even if our life didn't turn out like we pictured, the choices we make and the way we interact with others have an impact far larger than we may realise. Every scene in this film is iconic and memorable; there isn’t a second of boring, wasted time. Each line is perfectly crafted to show the motivations of the characters, which is amazing in a film with so much dialogue. As we follow George’s life, the characters that fill the gorgeous, ornate set pieces are all so three-dimensional, it's so easy to believe that each and everyone we see, no matter for how long, has an intricate past that weaves through the lives of George and their little town of Bedford Falls. Based on the 1943 book The Greatest Gift, the love and soul put into It’s a Wonderful Life is unmatched to this day. Iconic scenes are packed to the brim with lively extras talking and singing, giving a comforting Christmas ambience. Capra and Stewart brought to life the most likeable lead character in film history. There is no other character that I hoped would finally catch a break like George Bailey, and when it finally happens, it is shot so beautifully, in long, raw takes that let the moment breathe, letting the audience share a collective watery-eyed nod and smile. I believe the most important aspect of any film is to entertain the audience, but when a film can touch you in such a profound way while being entertaining, that is something special. There is no reason to wait until next Christmas season; you should watch this film immediately. I rate this film my first perfect 5/5.
