My review

Movie review - Wake Up Dead Man: A Knives Out Mystery Written by Evan Turok Rian Johnson’s Wake Up Dead Man (2025), the third installment of the Knives Out franchise. Starring Daniel Craig as the eccentric and brilliant detective Benoit Blanc, it sadly fell short of my expectations. Still a fun movie, but one that suffered from the success of the franchise’s past. I, being a huge fan of the original and very much enjoying the second, felt that Johnson failed to pull off the hat tick, and it doesn’t take a detective like Benoit Blanc to figure out why. Unlike the original, which jumped into the action in the very first scene, the first third of Wake Up Dead Man was incredibly slow and boring, sending the audience down a long, meandering path of unimportance that focused on characters who were, for the most part, one-dimensional. The best part of the Knives Out franchise is the lead character, Benoit Blanc (Daniel Craig), who is funny, witty, and lovable, always leaving me wanting when he isn’t on screen. Unfortunately, he was astonishingly relegated to a second lead in this film, taking a back seat to Jud Duplenticy (Josh O’Connor), a character still likeable and complex, but far less intriguing than Blanc. As the film began to focus more on the detective and the murder, the more it picked up steam, showing that Rian Johnson’s incredible mystery writing should always be the main event. Rian Johnson is a genius when it comes to writing a complicated murder mystery that cleverly incorporates all the sprinkles of evidence we are shown within the film, making sure each point is explained in a feasible manner. Incorporating comedy into a film with such a dark story can be a difficult task, but Johnson delivered flawlessly on every attempt. Taking risks and experimenting is the only way new and exciting films can exist, but I wish Wake Up Dead Man focused on the murder mystery itself, or at least on more well-written characters, as the past films did. An enjoyable film that left much in the tank.
