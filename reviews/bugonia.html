Movie Review - Bugonia Written by Evan Turok Bugonia (2025) is great, it’s a breath of fresh air and a shining beacon of creativity that has become so rare in recent years. Yorgos Lanthimos’ direction is superb, it always had me questioning characters’ true natures, intentions, and the entire premise of the world in which Bugonia takes place, yet I never felt lost or confused beyond the strategic leash Lanthimos allowed. A blend of modern dialogue and cinematography with the charm of a 60s sci-fi B movie makes Bugonia stand out as one of the best films of the year. The premise is established immediately, when we learn that Teddy (Jesse Plemons) and his cousin Don (Aiden DeLbis), are convinced that the billionaire pharmaceutical mogul Michelle Fuller (Emma Stone) is actually an alien sent down to eradicate the human race. We follow the kidnapping of Fuller and her attempt to convince a pair of mentally ill conspiracy theorists that she is not in fact an alien. Dramatic twists hold the suspense tight, like a rubber band stretching to its inevitable snap, revealing no one in Bugonia is who they seem. Although billed as a sci-fi/comedy, Bugonia is a suspenseful thriller at its core. My allegiances to characters never held for long, every time I thought I finally spotted the so-called “good guy,” I was tossed further into the layers of misdirection woven within. I commend Yorgos Lanthimos for his willingness to experiment and let the film be what it was unapologetically, while not making it cheesy or pretentious. Emma Stone is one of the most critically acclaimed actresses of all time, and shows exactly why that is in every single scene she is in. Jesse Plemons holds up his end of the deal too, his early kind personality never lets you see inside his real motives until the final act. Bugonia is a modern classic that I promise will be looked back on as inspiration for future directors to take an idea and run with it. A movie like this keeps you engaged and invested from the opening credits to the final scene. I give Bugonia a well-earned 4/5.
